人材募集Recruitment

Postgraduate student / Graduate student
Master’s programme / Doctoral programme

There are two admission routes: the Graduate School of Frontier Sciences (Department of Computational Biology and Medical Science) and the Graduate School of Medicine.
We welcome applicants who have an interest and enthusiasm for research in developmental biology, pluripotent stem cells, liver and pancreatic cancer, and cartilage, regardless of their previous research background.

Our diverse environment welcomes international students.
Please be advised by the following links: 
https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/adm/inbound/en/index.html
https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/about/intl_students_guide.html
